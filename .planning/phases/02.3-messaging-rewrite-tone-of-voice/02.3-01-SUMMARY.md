## Decisions Made

**Hero messaging approach:** Removed "TEMP-COAT TC101" as opening headline, replaced with "Tenkovrstvá tepelná ochrana pro průmysl a energetiku" / "Thin-film thermal protection for industrial facilities". This frames the site as solving a problem (heat losses) rather than selling a product.

**Industry descriptions vs. pills:** Changed from simple industry name pills to cards with title + 2-sentence description. Helps visitors self-qualify ("Does this apply to my sector?") without clicking through.

**Why UNION B+C visual distinction:** Used left-border accent (border-l-4) instead of circular icons to distinguish this section from other card-based sections (Problem, Solution, Target).

**Evaluation criteria placement:** Added 4 consultation workflow steps at bottom of Solution section to set expectations for how engagement works.
